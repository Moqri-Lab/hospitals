Exempt and possibly exempt hospitals

Hospitals that federal price transparency rules deem compliant, or may deem compliant, under 45 CFR 180.30(b). Hospital list: CMS Hospital General Information; beds: CMS Provider of Services (per CCN).

Exempt hospitals

Federally owned or operated hospitals (VA, DoD, Indian Health Service) are deemed compliant with price transparency rules (45 CFR 180.30(b)), so they are listed here without website or price-file fields and are not checked.

Possibly exempt hospitals

State-run psychiatric hospitals. A state forensic hospital that treats only people in the custody of penal authorities is deemed compliant (45 CFR 180.30(b)), but public data does not say which ones qualify, so they are listed here separately. Any cms-hpt.txt data found for them stays in catalog.csv.
